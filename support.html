Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tryal?

Tryal is a place to try out your content in front of fresh audiences. When you post, your video goes on Tryal: it is shown to viewers who don't know you, and their watching and reactions decide whether it survives. Posts that survive stay on your profile. Posts that don't survive quietly disappear, with no public record.

Will people I know see my posts?

While a post is being tested, Tryal keeps it away from people in your phone contacts (if you allow contacts access) and anyone on your "Hide my posts from" list. Once a post survives, anyone can see it. Tryal can't promise that no one you know will ever see a post, but it does not deliberately show your posts in testing to people you know.

When can I post?

Anytime. Reactions to other people's posts can be posted anytime too.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently deletes your account, posts, reactions, phone number and contacts data, and associated media. This action cannot be undone.

How do I report content or block a user?

Tap the flag icon on any post or reaction to report it. To block a user, visit their profile and tap "Block."

I'm having a technical issue — what should I include when I email you?

Please include: what device you're using, what you were trying to do, and (if possible) a screenshot or screen recording of the issue. This helps us fix it faster.
