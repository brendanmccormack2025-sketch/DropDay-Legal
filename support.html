DropDay Support Need help with DropDay? We're here for you. Contact Us For questions, bug reports, feedback, or account issues, email us at: DropDayApp@yahoo.com We aim to respond within a few business days. Frequently Asked Questions When can I post a Drop? Drops can be posted every night between 8 PM and 10 PM (your local time). Reactions to other users' drops can be posted at any time. How do I delete my account? Go to Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently deletes your account, posts, reactions, and associated media. This action cannot be undone. How do I report content or block a user? Tap the flag icon on any post or reaction to report it. To block a user, visit their profile and tap "Block." I'm having a technical issue — what should I include when I email you? Please include: what device you're using, what you were trying to do, and (if possible) a screenshot or screen recording of the issue. This helps us fix it faster. Legal Privacy Policy | Terms of Use
