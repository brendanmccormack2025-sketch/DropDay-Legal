Frequently Asked Questions

When can I post?

You can post anytime. Once posted, your content enters Trial — a testing period where it's judged by viewers before it earns a permanent place on your profile. Reactions to other users' posts can be posted at any time as well.

What is Trial?

Trial is a testing period every post goes through after you share it. Real viewers watch and react to your post, and based on that engagement, it either survives and stays on your profile, or it doesn't survive and is removed from public view. You can always see your own past posts and their outcome from your profile.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently deletes your account, posts, reactions, and associated media. This action cannot be undone.

How do I report content or block a user?

Tap the flag icon on any post or reaction to report it. To block a user, visit their profile and tap "Block."

I'm having a technical issue — what should I include when I email you?

Please include: what device you're using, what you were trying to do, and (if possible) a screenshot or screen recording of the issue. This helps us fix it faster.
